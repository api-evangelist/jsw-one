# JSW One Homes

> JSW One Homes is an end-to-end home construction service from the JSW Group, designed to make planning and building a home more transparent, reliable, and professionally managed.

## Summary
We help homeowners build durable, well-designed homes with confidence through architectural design, quality materials, verified contractors, and digital project tracking. Our integrated platform brings together home design, construction execution, and material assurance.

## Core Services
- **Home Design & Architecture**: Customized floor plans and 3D elevations.
- **Turnkey Execution**: End-to-end construction managed by experts.
- **Premium Materials**: Usage of JSW-grade steel, cement, and trusted materials.
- **Digital Tracking**: Milestone-based updates and digital project visibility.
